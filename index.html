AI Assistant

Your data is the context. Every time.

FinBuddy doesn't just bolt an AI onto a chat box. Before it answers any question, it looks at what you actually spent this month — your category totals, your budget limits, your recent transactions — and uses that as the starting point. So when you ask "where is my money going?", the answer is about your money, not a textbook example.

No internet? FinBuddy still answers. It falls back to a local AI, and if that's unavailable, it does the maths itself from your spending history and gives you a straight answer — never a "sorry, I can't help right now."
